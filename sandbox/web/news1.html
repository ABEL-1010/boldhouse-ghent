Ghent hosts a one-day AI agent hackathon

More than a hundred developers gathered at Boldhouse in Ghent on Saturday for a one-day hackathon focused on AI agents. Teams had seven hours to build working prototypes.

Organisers said the theme was trust: how people can rely on software that takes actions on their behalf. Several teams worked on tools that monitor what agents do.

Prizes were awarded for technical depth, real-world usefulness and the quality of the live demo.
